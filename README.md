# Awesome AI Ghostwriting Tools [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of AI and writing tools for professional ghostwriters who turn client interviews into finished books.

Tools for the whole ghostwriting workflow: capturing a client's stories and voice, outlining, drafting with AI, revising until the client signs off, checking originality, and delivering a formatted manuscript. AI drafts are a starting point; every chapter still needs the ghostwriter's and the client's review.

⭐ = recommended pick for working ghostwriters.

## AI Book Drafting Suites

Long-form platforms that can take an outline and interview notes to a full first draft.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Book workspace that plans a whole book, then drafts it chapter by chapter from the brief, with drafts ready for the ghostwriter's revision pass.
- ⭐ [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com/) - AI fiction suite with Story Bible, brainstorming, and prose drafting.
- [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Novel planning and prose workspace with a Codex for characters and facts.
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io/) - Book writing platform with AI drafting, outlines, and manuscript organization.
- [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai/) - Marketing-focused AI writer with brand voice settings, useful for thought-leadership and business books.

## Client Interviews and Transcription

Recording and transcribing client sessions so their stories and phrasing survive into the draft.

- ⭐ [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai/) - Live meeting transcription with speaker labels and searchable notes.
- [Descript](https://www.descript.com/) - Transcript-first audio and video editor for cleaning up interview recordings.
- [Riverside](https://riverside.fm/) - Remote recording studio with separate high-quality tracks and transcripts.
- [Rev](https://www.rev.com/) - AI and human transcription services for interview audio.
- [Happy Scribe](https://www.happyscribe.com/) - Transcription in many languages, handy for non-English clients.
- [Whisper](https://github.com/openai/whisper) - Open-source speech recognition model for transcribing interviews locally.
- [MacWhisper](https://goodsnooze.gumroad.com/l/macwhisper) - Mac app that runs Whisper on-device for private client recordings.

## Outlining and Book Structure

Turning hours of interviews into a chapter plan the client can approve.

- [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) - Visual outlining and timeline tool with story structure templates.
- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/) - Long-form writing app with binder, corkboard, and research folders for source material.
- [Milanote](https://milanote.com/) - Visual boards for sorting anecdotes, quotes, and chapter ideas.
- [Notion](https://www.notion.so/) - Flexible workspace for client briefs, interview logs, and chapter trackers.

## Voice, Style and Line Editing

Matching the client's voice and keeping prose clean across the manuscript.

- ⭐ [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) - Style and manuscript reports for repetition, pacing, and consistency.
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - Grammar, tone, and clarity suggestions with custom style guides.
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) - Readability checker that flags dense sentences and passive voice.
- [Writer](https://writer.com/) - Enterprise AI writing platform with enforceable style guides and terminology.

## Manuscript Rewrite and Revision

Reworking a full draft after client feedback, or rescuing a draft the client wrote themselves.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Manuscript Rewrite that polishes or restructures a full draft while keeping the author's voice.
- [AutoCrit](https://www.autocrit.com/) - Manuscript critique with genre-aware analysis of pacing and dialogue.
- [Marlowe (Authors.ai)](https://www.marlowe.ai/) - Analytical AI manuscript feedback on structure, pacing, and character.

## Series Bibles and Multi-Book Clients

Keeping names, facts, and arcs straight when a client signs on for a series.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Series story bible that carries characters, arcs, and open threads into the next volume.
- [NovelCrafter](https://www.novelcrafter.com/) - Codex shared across books so details and character progressions stay in sync.
- [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) - Cloud writing app with plot grids, story notes, and goals.

## Originality and AI Detection Checks

Many clients and publishers ask for an originality report before delivery.

- [Originality.ai](https://originality.ai/) - Plagiarism and AI content detection aimed at publishers and agencies.
- [Copyleaks](https://copyleaks.com/) - Plagiarism and AI content detection with document-level reports.
- [GPTZero](https://gptzero.me/) - AI text detector with sentence-level highlighting.

## Formatting and Export

Delivering the manuscript in the format the client, editor, or printer expects.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Export of the finished book to Word, EPUB, and Markdown.
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) - Cross-platform book formatter for ebook and print interiors.
- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) - Mac book formatting software with polished interior styles.
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free writing and formatting studio with EPUB and print-ready PDF export.
- [Pandoc](https://pandoc.org/) - Open-source document converter between Markdown, Word, EPUB, and PDF.
- [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) - Open-source ebook manager and converter for checking EPUB files.

## Covers and Titles

Title options and cover concepts to show the client early.

- ⭐ [ImagineYourBook](https://www.imagineyourbook.com/) - Cover and title generation matched to the manuscript.
- [BookBrush](https://www.bookbrush.com/) - Author graphics studio for covers, 3D mockups, and promo images.
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com/) - Design tool with templates for covers, one-sheets, and pitch decks.

## Client Management, Contracts and Payments

Proposals, contracts, milestones, and invoices for book-length projects.

- [Bonsai](https://www.hellobonsai.com/) - Freelancer suite for proposals, contracts, time tracking, and invoices.
- [HoneyBook](https://www.honeybook.com/) - Client flow platform with contracts, payment schedules, and scheduling.
- [Dubsado](https://www.dubsado.com/) - CRM with forms, contracts, and automated client workflows.
- [Calendly](https://calendly.com/) - Scheduling links for recurring client interview sessions.
- [Docusign](https://www.docusign.com/) - E-signatures for NDAs and ghostwriting agreements.

## Finding Ghostwriting Work

Marketplaces and professional networks where clients look for ghostwriters.

- [Reedsy Ghostwriters](https://reedsy.com/freelancers/ghostwriters) - Curated marketplace of vetted publishing professionals, including ghostwriters.
- [Association of Ghostwriters](https://associationofghostwriters.org/) - Professional community with resources and a directory for ghostwriters.
- [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/) - General freelance marketplace with steady book and ghostwriting jobs.
- [Fiverr](https://www.fiverr.com/) - Gig marketplace where ghostwriters package book services.
- [Authors Guild](https://www.authorsguild.org/) - Writers' organization with model contracts and legal guidance.

## General AI Assistants

General-purpose models ghostwriters use for summaries of interview transcripts, research, and brainstorming.

- [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) - Long-context AI assistant for working through full transcripts and chapters.
- [ChatGPT](https://openai.com/chatgpt/overview) - General AI chatbot for brainstorming angles, titles, and chapter hooks.
- [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) - Google AI assistant that works with Docs and Drive files.
